About

I'm a maker and digital designer who helps people build things they didn't think they could. My work runs from responsive redesigns to mobile apps to products that fold AI into everyday routines.

I hold a B.A. in Digital Design (Magna Cum Laude) from City College. Three instincts carry across everything here: reduce the cost of the first step, design the failure states, and make progress visible.

Every case here follows the same spine — Research → Pain points → IA → Wireframes → Validation — and each shipped as a working prototype, not just frames.

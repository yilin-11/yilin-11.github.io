About

I'm a maker and digital designer who helps people build things they didn't think they could. My work runs from responsive redesigns to mobile apps to products that fold AI into everyday routines.

I hold a B.A. in Digital Design (Magna Cum Laude) from City College. Three instincts carry across everything here: reduce the cost of the first step, design the failure states, and make progress visible.

Every case here follows the same spine — Research → Pain points → IA → Wireframes → Validation — and none of them stops at frames. Three shipped as interactive prototypes; the last two are full-stack apps, deployed and running.

Those two I designed and built with an AI coding agent. That changes the pace, not the job. Code arrives faster than anyone can read it, so the discipline moves to deciding what to keep: on Mutuo, nothing counted as fixed until a failing test said it was; on Foredge, every way the app can fail got a drawn state instead of a blank screen. I use AI to get to a running app — the decisions inside it are still mine to defend.

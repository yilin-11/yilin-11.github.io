[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) — free to use commercially and

Unsplash+ (paid) results were excluded deliberately: only photos the API flags as
free are here. **If you swap one out, check the `plus` / `premium` flags first** —
those two flags are the only thing separating a free photo from a licensed one.

All files are square 400px (dishes) or 200px (ingredients), JPEG q76-78, and every
`<img>` using them is `loading="lazy"` with an empty `alt` — they are decorative,
the row text carries the meaning.
